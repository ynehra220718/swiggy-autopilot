AI infusion for Swiggy · Product concept

Swiggy Autopilot
From meal intent to ready cart.

A constraint-aware ordering agent that converts people, budget, dietary needs and deadline into three complete, live-validated carts—with an all-in price and one-tap review.

Swiggy already helps users search. Autopilot closes the remaining gap: completing the decision confidently.

01

Specific user problem

High-intent users know their constraints—but must still navigate restaurants, menus, offers, portions and fees to assemble a valid answer.

Choice overload increases time and uncertainty. Scattered information creates product risk. A late change in payable total creates checkout hesitation.

JTBD: “When I’m hungry and tired, get me a dependable meal for my people, budget and time without making me research the marketplace.”

06

Validation and guardrails

Experiment hypothesis

Three complete, all-inclusive carts will reduce time-to-cart by ≥50% and improve order conversion by 3–5% relative, with neutral-or-better contribution margin and no increase in cancellations.

Live price integrityNo allergy inferenceSponsored labelsExplicit confirmation

Scope: MVP starts with voluntary entry, one city, dinner missions, one restaurant per cart and no autonomous payment. Targets are hypotheses, not claimed results.
